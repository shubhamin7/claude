## A2. Structure changes
- **Lambda/Anthropic (signed, now terminable).** Anthropic's S-1/A-2 (11/10) says the Aug-2026 Lambda agreement, about $34.9B of $41.3B total, "is currently terminable by either party" because Lambda did not secure the facility lease by the deadline. No termination notice has been delivered, and Anthropic says it does not intend to terminate. The commitment has not been cancelled, deferred or renegotiated, so Tripwire 7 is not fired. This is the closest call so far.
- **Nvidia/$500B financing platforms (Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, GS, KKR):** still MOUs from 10 Aug. No definitive agreements, sizes or first-loss terms found. Huang's stated backstop cap is up to $125B (25%). That figure is an announced intention, not a signed term.
- **Nvidia neocloud exposure:** third-party tallies range from ~$171.5B (CNBC via aiweekly) to $530.5B (Hunterbrook's reading of the 10-Q). These are secondary, and they wait on the Q3 FY27 10-Q, due after the **17 Nov** results.
- **CRWV funding mix is shifting:** an ATM equity program, plus contract-matched DDTLs (the $2.6B DDTL 5.5 was priced at SOFR+550, wider than the S+425–450 talk) instead of unsecured bonds at 16%.
- **Hyperscaler capex:** no cuts. META narrowed 2026 to $135–145B from $130–145B (8-K). AMZN held ~$220B and plans "meaningfully more" in 2027 (secondary). No useful-life changes found in Q3.
