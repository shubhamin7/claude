1. **H1 FY27 earnings call transcript (call held 19 Nov)**
   - Date: filed 25 Nov 2026, 19:06 IST (the previous evening, included).
   - Category: Primary-source management signal.
   - Source: [NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/TBICORN_25112026190628_TBI_Transcript.pdf)
   - Key datapoints:
     - Standalone revenue was ₹165 Cr and PAT ₹11.6 Cr, on 55,724 t processed in H1. Consolidated revenue was ~₹196 Cr (+43–44% YoY).
     - Standalone EBITDA was ~₹21 Cr, with margin ~12.8% (+306 bp half-on-half).
     - Maize prices hit a record ₹34 in Sangli in Oct–Nov because of a drought-hit crop. 3-month fixed-price contracts meant supply at below-spot prices (ITC cited), and buyers hesitated in Sep–Oct. Management expects normalisation from November and inventory gains in H2.
     - Ethanol demand for maize has fallen away. Smaller starch competitors are exiting, which management says is good for margins.
     - Core corn volume growth target is a ~35% CAGR. Utilisation is ~67% excluding TBI Maize.
     - The TBI Maize (JNPT SEZ) merger is blocked on the final occupancy certificate. Management now says "by December" but is hedging after previous misses.
     - A new Dubai subsidiary (100% owned by TBI Corn) has been set up.
   - **No mention of mainboard migration.** On the ROE question, this half shows earnings sensitivity to maize prices, which reads as cyclical.
