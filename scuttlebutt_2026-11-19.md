## What actually matters today

- **NAND is breaking from DRAM, so the memory thesis is now split.** DRAMeXchange (via MoneyToday, Nov 19) shows 512Gb TLC wafer spot **−24% m/m to $14.20** and 1Tb TLC **−14%**. DDR4 16Gb spot is −5% m/m at $79 (it peaked at $93 in early September), but DDR5 spot is holding above $60 and HBM/AI DRAM is effectively sold out through 2027. TrendForce sees **Q1'27 NAND contract −0% to −5% q/q**. That is bad for SNDK, mildly bad for MU's NAND business, and leaves the MU DRAM/HBM thesis intact.
- **SanDisk is relying on data-center contracts to offset the NAND decline** (Morgan Stanley APAC Summit, Nov 18–19). Data-center NAND is now about 50% of revenue (+1,700% y/y). Eight hyperscaler "new business model" contracts carry an aggregate **minimum 80% gross margin floor** and should be about two-thirds of FY28 revenue. Some customers have placed **deposits for 2029–31 supply**. Management is "not rushing" capacity and targets mid-to-high-teens bit growth. The 8-for-1 split takes effect at Friday's open.
- **Anthropic IPO priced at $83 (Nov 18 after the close) with a $26B strategic Class N placement.** Alphabet/Google bought **$10B**, Nvidia **$10B** and Micron **about $1B**. The stake ties GOOGL and NVDA more closely to a single major AI-compute buyer. For MU it is a small capital outlay but a strategic customer-alignment signal, and it set the risk-on tone for chips on Nov 19 (MU, MRVL and SNDK were up intraday).
- **New export-control rule took effect today (Nov 19).** Commerce's interim final rule requires licenses for advanced-AI-chip exports to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia, Brazil and others, and for offshore data centers serving China-headquartered firms. Existing facilities get 90 days to obtain one. NVDA's Q4 guidance already excludes China data-center compute, but Southeast Asia channel volumes are now at risk. MRVL and DELL face second-order exposure.
- **No greenfield memory-fab commissioning was reported in the window, so the thesis exit trigger was NOT hit.** SK Hynix Yongin phase-1 cleanroom remains targeted for Q1 2027 (per its F-1). Samsung P5 is targeting a 2028 ramp. YMTC Fab 3 in Wuhan is the main NAND-supply risk, and TrendForce cites it for NAND contract declines starting as early as Q2'27.

- **Date:** Nov 18 (21:30, article timestamp) / presentation Nov 19 (Asia)
- **Source:** [Investing.com transcript summary](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/sandisk-at-morgan-stanley-asia-pacific-summit-data-center-pivot-deepens-93CH-5037583)
- **Data-center mix:** data-center NAND revenue is up about 1,700% y/y and is now roughly 50% of the business. Management expects data center to exit 2026 as more than half of the total NAND market.
- **Contracts:** eight NBM contracts with hyperscalers carry a minimum 80% aggregate gross margin per year and are expected to make up about two-thirds of FY28 revenue. Some customers have placed deposits for 2029–2031 supply. The largest customers are AMZN, GOOGL and MSFT.
