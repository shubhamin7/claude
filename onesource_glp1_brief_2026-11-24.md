## Headline
**Health Canada authorized Lilly's Foundayo (orforglipron) on 13 Nov 2026 for obesity/overweight with comorbidity and for type 2 diabetes. Lilly expects it in Canadian pharmacies in January 2027.** This is the named priority trigger for OneSource's largest commercial DDC market. The 17 Nov brief reported "no approval found", so it missed this.
- **No Canadian price has been announced**, so dashboard #6 cannot be scored for Canada yet. Generic semaglutide pens sell uninsured at ~C$149–286/month (Felix, via Global News), versus ~C$78 on the Ontario public formulary.
- Oral Wegovy is still awaiting a Health Canada decision.

Separately, OneSource's **Chief Quality Officer (Bernhard Thurnbauer) resigned effective 17 Nov** (Reg. 30 filing). No reason or successor has been disclosed.

## Market-by-market

### United States
- **Rx data:** No November IQVIA weekly data was found. The latest is October: oral Wegovy ~179.5–184k Rx/wk and Foundayo ~58–65k Rx/wk. Sources conflict on week labels (Spherix / Investseekers / HLTH). IQVIA undercounts cash and telehealth channels (Citi, Novo CEO).
- **Oral share of new starts within the oral class:** the sources disagree, so all three are reported.
  - Novo says the pill took ~80% of new oral starts in Q3 (company claim).
  - Lilly says Foundayo took ~40% (company claim, IQVIA-based).
  - Jefferies puts the split at ~74% Novo / ~26% Lilly (analyst estimate).
- **Pricing:**
  - **Wegovy pill (NovoCare):** no direct-channel price change found this week. It remains $149–299/mo.
  - **Foundayo (LillyDirect self-pay):** $149–299/mo under current offers, which several secondary sites say end **31 Dec 2026**. 2027 pricing is unannounced; watch it.
  - **Wegovy injectable list price:** a secondary guide says the list price falls to ~$675/mo from 1 Jan 2027. **Unverified, and the announcement date is unclear.** It is not counted as a new development.
- **Payers:** No new PBM formulary decision found. Medicare Bridge (a $50 copay for Zepbound/Foundayo/Wegovy since 1 Jul) continues; its end date was not confirmed.
- **Novo US channel:** NovoCare was relaunched on 17 Nov as a support program with home delivery for insured and Medicare Bridge patients (Novo PR). This is access support, not a price change.
- **New oral approvals or filings:** none in the US this week. EU: the CHMP positive opinion for Foundayo came on 15 Oct, and an EC decision is expected ~mid-Dec. The EU is not a OneSource market.

## Thesis impact
**Facts:**
- An oral obesity GLP-1 is now authorized in Canada, with launch expected in January.
- Its price is unknown.
- Generic injectable semaglutide in Canada is priced at ~C$78 (public) to ~C$286 (uninsured) per month.

**Company claims:**
- Novo says ~80% of oral starters are new to treatment, which would mean the oral mainly adds patients.
- Truveta's earlier data (>1/3 naïve) is materially lower. The source-of-business question is therefore unresolved.

**Analyst speculation:** none new this week specific to Canada.

**Net:** this week does not by itself overturn the base case that absolute injectable volumes keep growing in OneSource's markets. No oral is launched or priced in Canada yet, and US-style innovator oral pricing ($149–349 cash) would still sit above Canadian generic pens. It does move Canada from "no oral" to "oral launching within ~6 weeks." That makes Foundayo's Canadian price, alongside Brazil's CMED price, the two numbers most likely to move indicator #6 in the coming weeks.
