# AI capex credit watch — 2026-11-18

**TRIPWIRES FIRED: #3 (Nvidia DSO), #7 (commitment deferral), #8 (Nvidia guarantees), #9 (Nvidia impairment); #1 continues.**
- **#3 DSO:** Nvidia's DSO was 45 → 60 → 64 days over Q1–Q3 FY27, two consecutive rises. It is also +21% YoY versus 53 days in Q3 FY26. Source: Nvidia CFO commentary.
- **#7 Deferral:** IREN said Microsoft-contracted capacity moves from 2026 to Jan/Feb 2027 at Microsoft's request. Nvidia's contingent neocloud agreements fell to $32B from $36B QoQ.
- **#8 Guarantees:** Nvidia's maximum guarantee exposure rose to $118.0B from $108.5B, including a new **$9.5B** "power and systems credit support" category. Beneficiaries are not named.
- **#9 Impairment:** Nvidia took a **$1.8B impairment** on non-marketable equity securities in Q3 FY27.
- **#1 continuing:** CRWV's 2032 notes are at ~14.2%, still >150bp above their late-September level of ~11.6–11.9%, although that is about 180bp tighter WoW.

## A2. Structure changes
- **Nvidia Q3 FY27 commitments** (primary, [CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm)):
  - Supply commitments rose to $390B from $279B.
  - Cloud service agreements plus not-yet-commenced DC leases rose to $102B from $74B. The leases run up to 20 years, and Nvidia says it "may assign" them to third parties.
  - Maximum guarantee exposure is $118.0B: SB Energy/OpenAI $105.0B, the new power and systems credit support $9.5B, and neocloud land/power/shell $3.5B. No guarantee payments have been made to date.
  - Contingent "neocloud agreements" are $32B, down from $36B. They were renamed from "AI cloud agreements". The reason for the $4B decline is not disclosed.
- **Nvidia leadership/policy:** Huang resigned 11/11 and Kress is interim CEO ([8-K 5.02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm)).
  - On the call, Kress said Nvidia is "rigorously evaluating future investments, commitments, and guarantees". She also said guarantees start only when projects are ready, so a delayed project drops out ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/data-center-capital-markets/nvidia-tops-forecasts-in-first-report-without-jensen-huang-but-gets-choosier-about-backing-data-centers)).
  - The WSJ (11/15) reports that Nvidia cut its Piketon/OpenAI lease commitment in August, size undisclosed, and paused some deals ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-financing-kress-neoclouds-upfront-payments)). This is secondary. The SB Energy maximum guarantee is unchanged at $105B.
- **Microsoft neocloud consolidation** ([CNBC 11/6](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/microsoft-moves-to-consolidate-its-relationships-with-neoclouds-in-ai.html)):
  - **IREN, company-disclosed (11/5):** capacity was handed over in August, but Microsoft requested a networking upgrade, which pushes the online date to Jan/Feb 2027.
  - **Nscale:** Microsoft walked from an England site; Microsoft's UK CEO said so on record. Separately, the FT reports Microsoft declined a Texas renewal.
  - **Oracle:** a deal was not renewed, per anonymous sources.
  - Microsoft says it is still "in the evaluation stage".
- **Lambda/Anthropic $34.9B:** still terminable but not terminated. No new filing or notice found.
- **Financing platforms** (Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, GS, KKR): still MOUs. No definitive agreements, sizing or first-loss terms disclosed.
