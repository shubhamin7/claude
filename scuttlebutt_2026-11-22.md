## What actually matters today

- **Memory pricing has turned, starting with NAND (thesis-critical).** TrendForce (Nov 19) cut its 4Q26 NAND contract forecast to **+8–13%** from +15–20%. It now sees **1Q27 NAND contract prices flat to −5% QoQ**, with every category falling from 2Q27. Drivers: NAND bit supply +25–30% in 2027 from QLC and higher layer counts, plus Chinese cloud providers cutting orders. GF Securities (Nov 17) made the same call: NAND surplus in 2027, falling prices from 2Q27, and SNDK the most exposed name. NAND spot prices are down about 20% in two weeks. DRAM 4Q26 is still +10–15% and TrendForce still calls DRAM tight through 2027. **SNDK's pricing thesis is now actively being challenged; MU is partly protected by DRAM and HBM.**
- **The AI-complex rout:** On **Fri Nov 20**, NVDA fell 14% to $125.53, MU about 14% to roughly $595 (lowest since May), and SNDK about 16% (worst in the S&P 500, on its first split-adjusted day). The Nasdaq fell 5.3% that day and about 10% for the week. Triggers: a **Reuters report that the SEC is probing Nvidia's accounting** (unconfirmed by the SEC or Nvidia), the fallout from Jensen Huang's Nov 12 resignation, and capital rotating into Anthropic's IPO. This is a price, sentiment and governance shock. **No demand or capex datapoint has been cut.**
- **Nvidia governance is the key swing factor this week.** **Nov 25** is the deadline for Nvidia to tell Treasury and the administration whether it used shell companies to evade China export controls. On Face the Nation (Nov 22, in window), Bessent said "he's on the outside now", which some read as softening on a Huang return. Kalshi odds of Huang returning rose from 7% to 12%. The board has said it has no intention of reinstating him.
- **Capacity timing: no exit trigger tripped.** Micron moved its first tool into the new **Singapore HBM advanced-packaging plant** on Nov 19, with first output still expected in early CY2027. This is Micron's own back-end HBM capacity, not greenfield wafer capacity from a competitor, so it is a watch item, not a trigger. No new SK Hynix Yongin, Samsung P5, Kioxia or YMTC commissioning news was found in the window.
- **Next catalysts:** **DELL reports FQ3 on Tue Nov 24.** Expect a read on its $95B AI backlog and on memory-cost pass-through; COO Clarke has said the constraint is "DRAM, DRAM, DRAM… NAND, NAND, NAND". MU reports Dec 17, guiding $61.5B ± $1.5B.
