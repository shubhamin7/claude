# OneSource (NSE: ONESOURCE) — Weekly Oral-vs-Injectable GLP-1 Brief — 2026-11-17

**Coverage window:** 10–17 Nov 2026. This is the first OneSource brief stored in this repo, so material items from 23 Oct to 9 Nov are included and marked **[pre-window, material]**.
**Method caveat:** The research used web search plus direct fetches of these primary documents: the Novo Q3 6-K, the Lilly Q3 10-Q (Note 9) and the Exame/DOU report of the ANVISA decision. The ANVISA database, CDSCO SEC minutes and the Health Canada DPD were not queried directly. The SFDA drug-list pages timed out on direct fetch, so the SFDA prices below come from search-result snippets of sfda.gov.sa and are **unverified**.

## Headline
**ANVISA approved oral Wegovy (semaglutide 1.5/4/9/25 mg tablets) for adult obesity in Brazil on 4 Nov 2026** (Resolução-RE nº 4.249, DOU). This is a named priority trigger for a OneSource market. There is no CMED price yet, and Novo guides to launch in 1H 2027. The label explicitly permits switching between oral and injectable "when clinically indicated." Separately, Novo's Q3 shows the US Wegovy franchise near route parity on prescriptions: 3.6M pill Rx vs ~4.1M injectable Rx in Q3, with US injectable Wegovy sales −13% CER. In International Operations (OneSource's arena), injectable semaglutide is still growing on volume.

## Market-by-market

### United States
- **IQVIA weekly (latest found: late Oct, via Fierce/analyst posts):** oral Wegovy ~183–184k Rx/wk vs Foundayo ~63–65k Rx/wk (week 29–30 of launch). **No November weekly data was found.** IQVIA undercounts telehealth/cash channels (Novo CEO claim).
- **Novo Q3 (4 Nov, 6-K) [pre-window, material]:**
  - Wegovy pill US sales were DKK 4,178M, with 3.6M Rx in Q3 and >8M Rx since the 5 Jan launch; >30% of weekly Rx are at the 9/25 mg doses.
  - Injectable Wegovy US was −13% CER, on ~4.1M Rx (~35% self-pay); Wegovy HD is ~5% of injectable Rx.
  - Ozempic US was −11% CER.
  - Novo says the Wegovy franchise holds ">50%" of branded obesity new patient starts in the US (company claim).
  - FY guidance was raised to −2% to +2% CER.
- **Lilly Q3 (29 Oct) [pre-window, material]:** Foundayo sales were $184M vs ~$229M consensus (StreetAccount). Lilly says Foundayo exited Q3 at ~40% of *oral* new starts, Rx and new patients doubled QoQ, and ~25% of patients are on maintenance doses. BofA had cited 27% of oral new starts pre-print, so the two sources differ.
- **Pricing (direct channels):** Wegovy pill self-pay is $149–299/mo (NovoCare); Foundayo LillyDirect self-pay is $149–349/mo. **No price changes found this week.** Medicare Part D obesity Bridge coverage has run since 1 Jul 2026.
- **New oral approvals or filings:** Lilly plans an FDA submission of Foundayo for OSA by YE2026 (ATTAIN-OSA positive, ~9 Nov). No new oral GLP-1 approvals this week.

### Canada
- **Oral Wegovy / Foundayo at Health Canada:** **no approval or NOC found.** Secondary sites say Foundayo has been under review since Jan 2026; no official confirmation. Priority trigger **not hit**.
- **Generic injectable semaglutide:**
  - Dr. Reddy's (approved 28 Apr) and Apotex (1 May) are on the market at ~C$78.14 per 4-week supply (Ontario formulary, Jun), vs Ozempic at ~C$228–240.
  - Sevmia (Apotex generic Wegovy) was authorized 29 Jun.
  - A Sandoz approval on 18 Sep is claimed by a single source and is **unconfirmed**.
  - Dr. Reddy's paused semaglutide supply pending validation of a revised API. Management guided a **November 2026 relaunch**, with Canada getting most initial supply under a 6–7M device target for H2 FY27 (company guidance). OneSource's Q2 commentary says Dr. Reddy's API "has commenced," supporting the relaunch.
- **pCPA / provinces:** nothing new. No public Wegovy coverage for weight; Quebec declined (Apr). A Saskatchewan 2027 review is unconfirmed.
- **Novo response:** Novo reports lower realized prices in Canada with volumes "broadly stable" (Q3 6-K, company claim).
- **Supply/recall/quality:** none found beyond the Dr. Reddy's API revalidation above.

### Brazil
- **🚩 ANVISA approved oral Wegovy on 4 Nov 2026** (DOU Resolução-RE nº 4.249) [pre-window, material, priority trigger]:
  - Doses 1.5/4/9/25 mg; adults only, at BMI ≥30 or ≥27 with a comorbidity.
  - Novo has applied to CMED for a price. Launch is guided for "coming quarters," and InfoMoney/Agência GBC report 1H 2027.
  - **No Foundayo filing news found.**
- **Goldman Sachs view (via InfoMoney, 4 Nov; analyst opinion):** the oral expands the market and mainly captures *new* GLP-1 users rather than switching pen patients. Goldman's medium-term risk is slower growth for Hypera's Semavy pen, with little near-term impact.
- **Generic pens:**
  - EMS Ozivy is priced at R$452–498 per pen, plus a 3-pen program at R$999.
  - Hypera's Semavy sold ~93k units in its first month (Goldman); Hypera's Zempneo was approved 29 Jul.
  - Dr. Reddy's has ANVISA approval, with launch guided for "beginning of Q4" FY27 (Jan–Mar 2027).
  - Biomm/Biocon status is unclear.
- **Innovator pricing:** Lilly cut Mounjaro ~35% via combo packs/loyalty program (Jun 2026, background). Novo cut Ozempic/Wegovy 20% in 2025. **Nothing new this week.**

### India
- **Oral Wegovy (obesity dose):** CDSCO's SEC sought more data from Novo, including Indian BE and formulation evidence. **Not approved.** Nothing new this week.
- **Foundayo India:** **no CDSCO approval, launch or price found.** The CRITICAL trigger (launch within ~1.5x of generic pen price) is **not hit**.
- **Generic pen market:**
  - Pharmarack, via Business Standard (13 Oct): total semaglutide volume rose from ~114k units in Feb to ~434k in Sep. Generics are ~74% of volume, and the market is "plateauing" into steady growth.
  - Dr. Reddy's sold ~442k units through Sep and Sun ~151k (Reuters, 23 Oct). Dr. Reddy's rolled out new brands because supply of its original Obeda brand was disrupted until November.
  - Generic pens are still the lowest-priced option at ~₹1,290/month. **No new price cuts were found.** ~12 makers sell ~20 brands.
- **Generic oral semaglutide (diabetes dose):** no new uptake data found. No obesity-dose oral generic was identified.

## Thesis impact
**Facts:**
- In International Operations, injectable semaglutide grew on volume in Q3 (IO injectable Wegovy +16%, Ozempic +8% at CER).
- India generic pen volumes roughly quadrupled Feb→Sep.
- The new oral approvals in OneSource markets (Brazil; Foundayo in KSA/UAE) are not yet priced at or near generic-pen levels where prices are visible.

**Company claims:**
- Novo says its pill mostly draws GLP-1-naïve patients and expands rather than cannibalizes.
- Lilly says Foundayo holds ~40% of oral new starts.
- Neither company disclosed a switching rate.

**Analyst speculation:**
- Goldman expects Brazil's oral to slow branded-generic pen growth over time, mainly by capturing new users.

**Net:** this week does not overturn the base case that absolute injectable volumes keep growing in OneSource's markets. The US data (Novo injectables down in value, Wegovy pill near half of Wegovy Rx) shows the direction of travel if orals reach IO at low prices. That makes the Brazil CMED price and the Saudi price ratio the key items for the coming weeks.
