## What actually matters today
- **Memory pricing has turned at the margin (core thesis signal).** TrendForce (Nov 25) says some Chinese CSPs **cut 2027 memory orders**. It trimmed its 2027 NAND revenue forecast to about $419B (+29%) and 2027 DRAM to about $1.15T (+69%). It now sees Q1'27 NAND contract prices **flat to −5% QoQ**. DRAM stays tight through 2027, but price increases narrow and could plateau in 2H27. BNP Paribas pulled its forecast for DRAM/NAND supply-demand balance forward to **mid-2027 (from mid-2028)**. NAND spot is already falling: a 512Gb wafer is about $14.30, −12% WoW (BofA). This is the clearest negative datapoint for SNDK so far and a yellow flag for MU.
- **Sell-side capitulation on memory.** BNP downgraded MU to Neutral and cut its target to $775 from $1,700. UBS cut MU to $1,100 from $1,625 (still Buy). Evercore cut SNDK to $175 from $200 (still Outperform; the figures are post-split). MU fell about 11% Tue and about 7% Wed, to roughly $587. SNDK fell about 11% Tue and about 8–10% Wed, to roughly $70; it is down about 45–50% over a month.
- **Dell flatly contradicts the memory-glut narrative.** DELL management said on the call that "nothing… suggests" enterprise server DRAM/NAND prices will decline, and expects server pricing to stay inflationary into 2027. Mizuho expects the component supply gap to *worsen* in CY27. Dell's own print was huge: FQ3 revenue $54.9B (+103%), AI orders $60.2B, AI backlog **$132.3B** (from $95B), FY27 guidance raised to $202B, and $25B added to the buyback. The stock still fell, and analysts cut targets on multiple compression and memory-cost margins.
- **No greenfield commissioning trigger tripped.** Latest timing: SK Hynix Yongin Ph1 cleanroom opens around Feb 2027, with production targeted for about Oct 2027 and tool orders reportedly placed (about ₩31T). Samsung P5 Fab 1 targets 2028. These dates are not new in-window and none of the fabs is commissioning now. Nothing found on Kioxia or YMTC.
- **Nvidia governance overhang.** S&P put NVDA's AA rating on **CreditWatch Negative** on Nov 25 (risk of a one-notch cut within 90 days). It cited weaker governance after the board reinstated Jensen Huang, plus potential export-control issues. Bipartisan calls for an investigation are growing. NVDA fell 5.7% Tue and about 4–7% Wed, giving back much of Monday's +31%.
